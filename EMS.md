**Company Employee Management System**

The Company Employee Management System is a centralized web-based platform developed to streamline employee management, project collaboration, HR operations, payroll, attendance, and other internal company processes. The system supports three user roles like Admin/HR, Project Manager, and Employee and each with role-specific permissions and functionalities.

1\. Role-Based Access Control and Profile Management (Abir)  
Provides secure authentication and restricts system access based on user roles (Admin/HR, Project Manager, and Employee), ensuring each user can access only authorized modules and functionalities. Maintains employee records, including personal information, contact details, qualifications, employment history, profile pictures, and other relevant information.

2\. Employee Onboarding Process (Arefin)

Manages the onboarding process for newly hired employees, including document submission, account creation, department assignment, project allocation.

3\. Company Announcement & Knowledge Management (Mamun)

Allows Admin/HR to publish company-wide announcements, policy updates, holiday notices, and important information. It also provides a centralized knowledge base for company policies, coding guidelines, project documentation, and onboarding resources.

4\. Leave Application & Approval (Mamun)  
   
Allows employees to submit leave requests while enabling Project Managers and HR to review, approve, reject, and provide comments on applications.

5\. Work-from-Home Request Management (Mamun)  
   
Allows employees to submit work-from-home requests, which can be reviewed, approved, or rejected by Project Managers or HR.

6\. Employee Attendance Tracking (Abir)

Records employee attendance through daily check-in and check-out, maintains attendance history, and calculates working hours.

7\. Salary Management & Payslip Generation (Abir)

Manages employee salaries, bonuses, deductions, and payroll while automatically generating downloadable monthly salary slips.

8\. Loan Application System (Mamun)

Allows employees to apply for company loans by selecting repayment periods. HR can review applications, approve or reject requests, and provide comments.

9\. Performance Evaluation System (Arefin)

Enables Project Managers to assess employee performance through ratings, reviews, and feedback while maintaining historical evaluation records.

10\. Employee Complaint & Grievance Management (Arefin)

Provides employees with a platform to submit workplace concerns or complaints and track their review and resolution by HR.

11\. Project Creation & Management (Arefin)

Allows Admins to create and manage projects, assign Project Managers and employees, monitor project progress, team participation, and project analytics. 

12\. Project Tracking using Kanban Board (Abir)

Tracks project tasks using a Kanban workflow with stages such as To Do, In Progress, Testing, and Completed, enabling teams to monitor task progress efficiently.

13\. Project-Based Channel Chat System (Abir)

Provides Slack-like communication channels within each project (e.g., \#general, \#frontend, \#backend, and \#database) to support real-time team collaboration.

14\. Meeting Room Booking System  (Mamun)

Allows employees to view room availability, reserve meeting rooms, manage bookings, and cancel reservations when necessary.

15\. Company Calendar (Abir)

Provides a centralized company calendar to manage holidays, meetings, project deadlines, company events, and other important schedules.

16\. Poll System (Project-Based & Company-Wide)  (Arefin)

Allows Project Managers to create project-specific polls while enabling Admin/HR to conduct company-wide surveys, voting, and employee feedback collection.

17\. Notification System (Abir)

Delivers real-time notifications for important activities such as leave approvals, work-from-home requests, project assignments, meeting reminders, salary generation, announcements, and other system events.

18\. Training & Project Certificate Generation (Arefin)

Automatically generates digital certificates for employees upon successful completion of projects. Certificates recognize employee contributions and are stored within employee profiles for future reference.

19\. Company Finance Management (Arefin)

Manages payroll expenses, operational costs, project budgets, and financial reports to support company budgeting and financial planning.

20\. Reports & Analytics Dashboard (Abir)  
Provides interactive dashboards and analytical reports that visualize attendance, payroll, employee performance, project progress, workforce statistics, and other key organizational metrics through charts and graphs.
